Our Story

A Clearer Way to Explore Property

We built Lumeridge to make browsing property feel organized, transparent and personal — with honest information and an easy way to ask questions.

“Every listing deserves the same clarity — and every visitor deserves honest information.”

Which areas do you cover?

Our collection focuses on four South Florida markets: Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach — from urban condominiums to waterfront homes and commercial space.

How does the property search work?

Search the full collection from any page. You can filter by listing type, location, property type, price range and bedrooms, and sort results.

Can I save properties?

Yes. Tap the heart on any listing. Saved properties are stored in your browser so you can compare them later on the Saved Properties page.

How do I ask about a specific property?

Every listing page has its own inquiry form with the property already selected. Choose whether you want more information, a viewing or pricing details.
