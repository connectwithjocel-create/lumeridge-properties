Who We Are

About Lumeridge Properties

A South Florida real estate brand built around curated listings, clear information and an easy inquiry experience.

Brand Introduction

Find Where Life Fits.

Lumeridge Properties is presented as a Miami-based real estate brand focused on residential discovery, luxury homes, rentals and selected commercial properties across South Florida.

We believe finding a property should feel organized and calm — so every listing is presented beautifully, search is simple and inquiries are effortless.

Our Mission

Make Property Discovery Feel Considered

Present every property with the same clarity, help visitors narrow their options quickly, and make it easy to ask the right questions — whether they are buying a first condo, renting near work or searching for a waterfront home.

Our Approach

How We Think About Property Discovery

  • Start with what matters to the visitor: location, budget, space
  • Show consistent specifications so comparisons are fair
  • Present photography and details honestly
  • Offer guides that answer common Florida-specific questions
