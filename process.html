How It Works

Our Process

Four steps from first search to first conversation — explore, compare, inquire and connect.

  1. STEP 01

    Explore

  2. STEP 02

    Compare

  3. STEP 03

    Inquire

  4. STEP 04

    Connect

Step 01

Explore

Start broad. Use the search panel or category navigation to see what each market offers, then narrow by listing type, location, type, price and bedrooms.

  • Filter by city and property type
  • Browse categories and collections
  • Read buyer and renter guides
Step 02

Compare

Save properties that fit and compare them on consistent specifications — size, bedrooms, parking, year built and features.

  • Save favorites with one tap
  • Review galleries and amenities
  • Note questions for each listing
Step 03

Inquire

Use the property-specific inquiry form to ask about availability, viewings or pricing. Your property of interest is included automatically.

  • Property-specific forms
  • Clear inquiry types
  • Instant on-screen confirmation
Step 04

Connect

We follow up to arrange viewings, answer questions and guide your next steps.

  • Viewing coordination
  • Introductions to lenders and inspectors
  • Next-step guidance
